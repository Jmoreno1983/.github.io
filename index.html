Services

Inspection and testing for structural steel

Shop and field inspection performed to your project specifications and the governing codes, including AWS D1.1/D1.8, with clear, documented results.

VT / CWI

Weld Inspection

Visual inspection of structural welds before, during, and after welding, including WPS and welder qualification review.

UT

Ultrasonic Testing

Ultrasonic examination of complete joint penetration groove welds and other welds requiring volumetric testing.

MT

Magnetic Particle Testing

Detection of surface and near-surface discontinuities in welds and base metal.

PT

Liquid Penetrant Testing

Detection of surface-breaking discontinuities.

BOLTING

High-Strength Bolting Inspection

Verification of bolt markings, installation, and tensioning of high-strength structural bolted connections.

QA / QC

Shop & Field Quality Assurance

Fabrication and erection inspection support, with reports your project team can rely on.
